What I am working on right now

The flood mapping project that takes up most of my week, where I would like it to go next, and the papers that got me here.

Featured · Ongoing

Flood mapping and the reliability of ground truth

Real World AI Lab (RAIL), National University of Singapore
Advisor: Dr. Sanka Rasnayaka
  • I sit on the ground truth side, building and comparing different ML algorithms against the reference data.
  • The models pick flooding out of radar imagery, mostly SAR, then get scored against reference flood maps.
  • Here those references are the official flood outlines drawn by CEMS, and everyone treats them as correct.
  • We are checking whether they really are, and what happens to reported accuracy once you stop assuming it.

Where I want to take this next

The question underneath my current project, how much of a reported error belongs to the reference data rather than the model, is one I would rather spend several years on than a few months. It shows up everywhere once you start looking for it, and hardly anyone wants to touch it.

So I am looking for a PhD position in computer vision, diffusion models, remote sensing, machine learning evaluation, or data quality for scientific pipelines. If you supervise in any of those areas, or you know a group that is recruiting, I would genuinely like to hear from you.
